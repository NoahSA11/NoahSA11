# Three ideas considered

Context: Noah is repositioning into Marketing Ops / RevOps / GTM Engineering, is job hunting,
builds tools for his own problems first, and already ships React + Supabase + Vercel.
Goal: spend ~$100 of expiring Claude credit on something he will actually use and show off.

## 1. Pipeline of One — a GTM engine pointed at his own job search  (WINNER)
Treat the job hunt as a sales pipeline. Ingest GTM/RevOps job postings from public ATS feeds
(Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby), dedupe, score fit against an ICP-style rubric of his profile,
draft tailored outreach for human approval, track a kanban funnel, email a weekly digest.
- Use: every day, during the job search.
- Signal: the artifact IS the portfolio. It proves lead sourcing, enrichment, scoring,
  sequencing, and funnel reporting — the exact job he is applying for.
- Credit fit: big enough to absorb a long Opus build (ingest, scoring, UI, tests, deploy).

## 2. Afterschool Club v2 — AI lesson generator + teacher dashboard
Extend his live, real-user app: teachers paste a topic, get a Hebrew-UI reading lesson with
word lists and pronunciation exercises; dashboard shows per-kid progress.
- Strong because it has real users. Weaker for the GTM career pivot, and the source is
  private/whitelabeled, so it is hard to showcase.

## 3. Personal "second brain" digest — Gmail/Calendar/Notion daily brief agent
A scheduled agent that reads his inbox, calendar and notes, and sends one morning brief.
- Useful, but generic, crowded space, and weak portfolio signal.

## Pick: #1. Reasons: (a) daily use, (b) directly demonstrates target-job skills,
(c) publicly shareable with no private customer data, (d) clear done-criteria.
